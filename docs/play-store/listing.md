## Full description

Experience the Living Word with The Word App, a Bible companion designed to help you read, understand, remember, and share Scripture.

Read the Bible with offline-first access, follow a daily reading plan, and return each day for Scripture and devotional reflection. Save meaningful passages, build Scripture Memory habits, capture sermon notes and recordings, and create shareable verse designs in Word Studio.

Features include:

- Bible reading with multiple translations
- Daily verses and promise-based reflections
- Guided devotional content
- A Bible-in-one-year reading plan
- LOGOS Notes for sermon notes and recordings
- Scripture Memory with active-recall exercises
- Gospel tracts and Word Studio sharing tools
- Search, saved verses, narration, and personal progress
- Local scheduled reminders that continue without an account

Most personal content remains on your device. No account is required.

The Word App helps you move beyond simply reading Scripture toward understanding it, remembering it, and applying it each day.
