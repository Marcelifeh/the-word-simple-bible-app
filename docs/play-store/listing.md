## Full description

**Read. Reflect. Remember. Live the Word.**

The Word App is a Scripture-centered Bible companion designed to help you move beyond simply reading the Bible and build a consistent life in God's Word.

Read Scripture with offline-first access, follow a daily reading plan, and return each day for a verse, promise, and devotional reflection. Save meaningful passages, strengthen Scripture Memory, capture sermon notes and recordings, and create designs that make God's Word easy to share.

**Read and understand Scripture**

- Read the Bible in multiple translations
- Search for a verse, word, or passage
- Continue from your last reading position
- Explore clear verse insights and commentary
- Listen with device narration and supported Bible audio

**Build a daily rhythm**

- Begin with a Daily Verse and Today's Promise
- Follow the Bible-in-one-year reading plan
- Work through guided devotionals and reflections
- Track Bible reading days and weekly spiritual activity
- Set local reminders for Scripture, devotionals, prayer, and reflection

**Remember and apply the Word**

- Save verses, notes, highlights, and favorites
- Review Scripture with active-recall memory sessions
- Keep devotional journal entries and prayer reflections
- Return to completed devotionals and saved insights

**Capture what God is teaching you**

- Use LOGOS Notes for sermon notes and timestamps
- Record sermon audio locally on your device
- Detect Scripture references inside sermon notes
- Export and share notes when you choose

**Create and share**

- Design Scripture and Gospel tract graphics in Word Studio
- Use built-in styles or a private photo from your device
- Adjust layout, image fit, zoom, and text readability
- Download or share the finished design

Most personal content remains on your device. No account is required, and local reminders continue to work offline.

The Word App helps you read Scripture, reflect on truth, remember God's Word, and carry it into everyday life.
